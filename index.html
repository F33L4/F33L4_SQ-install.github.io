About the project

This website serves as a structured presentation of the design, implementation and documentation of the entire audio system.

System overview

Fully active multi-way car audio system designed for sound quality. The front stage uses a 3‑way configuration with midbass drivers in the doors and midrange + tweeters installed in custom A‑pillar pods.

Subwoofer concept

The system uses two subwoofers with different roles. A front subwoofer covers approximately 55–100 Hz to support the front stage, while a rear subwoofer is dedicated to deep bass reproduction below roughly 55 Hz.

Optimization

The system was tuned using DSP crossover filters, time alignment and parametric equalization. The final frequency response follows the target curve and covers approximately 12 Hz – 20 kHz inside the vehicle.

Engineering tools and measurements

Software tools used during the design, modeling and tuning process:

Helix DSP PC-Tool Room EQ Wizard (REW) HOLMImpulse SpeakerBox enclosure modeling software WinISD enclosure modeling software

Measurements were performed using a calibrated UMIK‑1 measurement microphone with calibration file. Frequency response, phase and time alignment were analyzed and optimized using REW and HOLMImpulse.

The electrical system of the vehicle was also upgraded. The battery-to-chassis, alternator-to-battery and engine-to-battery wiring was upgraded to 35 mm² OFC cable. The audio power path uses a 125 A main fuse located at the battery, followed by a 25 mm² OFC power cable and a fused distribution block. Individual fused outputs power each device: 80 A for the Audison AF M1D (subwoofer amplifier), 60 A for the Zapco HB 154D II, 30 A for the Fosi Audio CA30 and 15 A for the Helix DSP.3S.

The sum of fuse ratings in the distribution block exceeds the main fuse rating because the main fuse protects the power cable, while individual fuses protect the connected devices. Due to the dynamic nature of music and frequency band separation between amplifiers, the system never reaches maximum current draw on all channels simultaneously.
